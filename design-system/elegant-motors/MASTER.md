# Elegant Motors — Design System Master

## Brand
- Logo: chrome/silver car silhouette + ELEGANT MOTORS on black (`/brand/logo.png`)
- Owner: Dany Roukoz — portrait `/brand/dany-roukoz-editorial.jpg` (Higgsfield Soul 2 from reference)
- Atmosphere: `/brand/showroom-hero.jpg` (Higgsfield Soul Location)

## Visual direction
- Full dark cinematic showroom (not light SaaS)
- Motion-driven: GSAP ScrollTrigger hero parallax + section reveals + inventory stagger
- Portfolio-grid inventory with image-first tiles and chrome chip filters
